About

My story.

I'm Kelvin Larbi Yeboah a business and data analyst who believes every dataset tells a story worth understanding. My journey started with curiosity: why do some businesses thrive while others stall? Data, I discovered, holds the answer.

I help organizations translate messy, scattered information into clear actionable business insight. From cleaning raw data with SQL and Python to building dashboards leadership actually uses, my work sits at the intersection of business strategy and analytical rigor.
